# Kalpi — PoC bootstrap playbook

Virality after scale is not the problem. Reaching the first credible pocket of activity is.

## The bootstrap wedge

Do not launch to “young Israelis.” Start with a coordinated seed:

- **10 creators** whose audience overlaps 18–24.
- **3 formats:** one short vertical walkout, one WhatsApp still, one “guess the quote before the name” prompt.
- **One launch window:** creators open during the same 60–90 minutes so the format feels active without altering numbers.
- **One common product:** creator links identify acquisition source, not political profile. Everyone sees the same editorial sequence.

Creator URL:

`http://host/?ref=creator-name`

Exact-card URL:

`http://host/?card=DEM-Q-01&ref=creator-name`

Duplicate gift/trade preview:

`http://host/?gift=DEM-Q-01&ref=creator-name`

The gift URL demonstrates social intent. It does not transfer ownership in the PoC.

## The artifact must carry the loop

Every share artifact needs:

1. The quote/fact large enough to read in-feed.
2. Identity and card finish.
3. A source cue.
4. The advocacy disclosure.
5. A reason to tap: “See the line before the name.”
6. A deep link to the exact walkout.

The artifact is unsuccessful if it only says “I pulled a rare.” The quote is the conversation; rarity earns attention.

## Instrument the actual funnel

Server-recorded events:

`pack_opened → back_completed → source_opened → binder_reached → share_created → referral_opened`

Secondary:

`gift_preview_created`

PoC learning thresholds:

- 10 / 10 seeded creators complete one pack.
- ≥70% of opened packs reach all six backs.
- ≥30% of seeded players create a share artifact.
- ≥20% of referral visitors start a guest session.
- ≥15% of completed backs open the inspectable source during the seeded test.

These are thresholds for diagnosing the PoC, not public traction claims.

## Failure tree

### Creators do not post

- The artifact requires too much explanation → make the quote/name reveal self-contained.
- The disclosure overwhelms the visual → shorten, do not hide.
- The asset is not native to their channel → export 9:16 video frame + 4:5 still + WhatsApp still.

### People view but do not tap

- Quote has no curiosity gap → choose a clearer sourced line.
- Identity is already fully revealed in the caption → let the walkout finish the reveal.
- CTA is generic → deep-link to “guess who said it,” not the homepage.

### People tap but do not open a pack

- Onboarding delays the pack → advocacy disclosure stays visible but concise; pack remains first action.
- The shared card cannot be previewed → exact-card walkout opens before asking for a session action.
- Mobile load is slow → compress art; CSS motion first; no autoplay video dependency.

### Packs open but backs do not complete

- Walkout feels slow instead of suspenseful → shorten minimum holds, preserve tap control.
- Back is too dense → keep 40–60 words and one “why it matters.”
- Source looks like legal text → use a short source label plus one inspect link.

### Shares do not create referrals

- Share is only collection vanity → put the quote and source cue back at the center.
- No social mechanic after duplicates → expose gift/trade preview.
- Activity looks empty → coordinate seed timing; never invent totals.

## Privacy and credibility

- Sharing is optional.
- A share card need not display a user’s faction.
- Referral codes identify the creator/link, not inferred ideology.
- Public activity is aggregate event count only.
- Demo fixtures must say “demo.” Production-looking fake totals are forbidden.

## After the PoC

If the funnel works, build real gifting/trading only after abuse, consent, and ownership rules are specified. If the funnel does not work, do not hide the failure behind a faction board; fix the walkout or artifact first.
