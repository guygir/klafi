# Why a digital collectible — and what we steal

Kalpi is not “a civic explainer with cards on top.” It is a **digital collectible** whose payload is lists and platforms. This note is the maneuver: why that format works, and which collectible rules we take.

Full product rules: [gdd-lite.md](gdd-lite.md).

---

## 1. Pokémon TCG Pocket = proof the digital maneuver works

Physical TCGs already work on this age. **State Makers / יוצרים מדינה** (Piece of History) proved cards can teach Israeli history: 98-card set, packs of 8, holos, first edition sold out. [pieceofhistory.com/statemakers](https://www.pieceofhistory.com/statemakers). Do not compress that to “cards work.” It is the local analog.

The remaining question is: **why digital, and why collect-first?** Pocket (Creatures / DeNA / TPC, launched 30 Oct 2024) is the current answer.

### Five advantages we take from Pocket

| # | Pocket does | Why it matters | Kalpi |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Daily timed packs** | A clock, not a pile. Encourages return | One free pack / 24h |
| 2 | **The rip is a ritual** | Anticipation → tear → pause. Addictive on purpose | Tear → spread → rare pause → flip the back |
| 3 | **Collecting sets is progression** | Card lands in a hole. Chase the binder, not a duel | Party sets + commons, dashed wells |
| 4 | **Rarity is a screenshot** | Holo / rare is spectacle. That is the virality | Share the pull; daily numbered holo |
| 5 | **Quick sessions** | Phone / bus. Not a 30-minute table match | Default under 90 seconds |

We do **not** take the shop, hourglasses, or battles. Extra pack = quiz.

**It is collection-first, not battle-first.**  
Naavik: Pocket “freely distribut[es] card packs through simple daily logins rather than requiring match participation, emphasizing collecting as the main attraction.” Battles exist; they are not the door. That is our shape: **no battles at all.** We copy the door.

**It was built for people who never played the physical TCG.**  
Cut or Tap (Gearhart, Nov 2024): earlier digital Pokémon (PTCGO, TCG Live) ported the long table game and **abandoned collecting**. They failed to grow a new mobile audience. Pocket optimized for the phone: 10–15 minutes on a bus, not a 30-minute match. Our 18–24s will not sit a civics lecture. They will rip a pack.

**The numbers show the loop scales.**  
~150M downloads, on the order of **$1.3B** in year one; collection-first social (binders, rare shares) is a stated growth driver. We do not need their revenue. We need the proof that **daily digital rips + a binder** can become a habit for this generation.

**The ritual is the product.**  
Arcader / Polygon preview: swipe-to-tear, sound designed by opening real packs, “a little daily burst of serotonin.” Creatures’ art director: they expected **daily** opens, so they obsessed over the tear sound. If Kalpi’s rip is a fade-to-grid, we did not ship a collectible.

**After the rip, the card must land in a set.**  
Naavik: sequences that show **where the new card slots into the collection**, plus milestone rewards for completion. Chase is “collect ’em all,” not “win the duel.” Our binder dashed wells are that beat: missing knowledge, not missing DPS.

**Social collecting is the virality, not chat.**  
Wonder Pick, binder showcases, “thanks” on someone else’s pull (PocketGamer / GFRFund). Players advertise the game by showing a rare. Our share-the-pull and camp energy board are the civic version. We do **not** take paid Wonder-Pick rewinds or hourglasses that skip the timer.

**Digital does things print cannot.**  
Official Pocket launch: parallax, immersive cards, effects that only exist on a screen. Our daily numbered holo (`#1 of 1 today`) is the civic use of “no print cap.”

**What Pocket proves, in one line for the deck:**

> A digital TCG works when the daily pack is free enough to become a habit, the rip feels physical, the card has a hole in a binder, and showing the rare is the social game. Battles are optional. Money is optional. We take the first four and spend the extra pack on a quiz.

## 2. Digital-collectible musts (what the field actually does)

Drawn from Pocket analyses, digital rip products (e.g. Blez: pick / rip / reveal), and standard gacha/collectible hygiene. Mapped to Kalpi.

### Take

| Must | Why it exists | Kalpi |
|---|---|---|
| **The rip is a ritual** | Anticipation → tear → pause on rare. Variable reward. | Tear → spread → rare pause → **tap to flip the back** |
| **Timer, not a pile of packs** | Daily/12h gate makes a habit. Pocket: free pack on a clock. | One free pack / 24h. No hourglass shop. |
| **Card lands in a hole** | Completion is visible. “Collect ’em all.” | Binder wells, set %, party sets + commons |
| **Rarity ladder** | Chase without needing PvP power. | C / U / R / Holo + daily numbered date |
| **Pity / a floor** | Trust. Civic games cannot withhold knowledge like a casino. | 3 packs without a new-to-them card → guarantee one |
| **Show the rare** | Binders and share images are the ad. | Share pull; camp energy is public spectacle. [outbid.lol](https://outbid.lol) proved people **pay** to sit on a public ladder — we invert: nobody pays |
| **New set so you are never “done”** | Pocket drops expansions ~monthly. | New lists after 9 Sept; optional CEC pulse, not polls |
| **Transparent odds** | Blez: showing tiers is the opposite of a dark loot box. | Publish pack mix (3C / 2U / 1R-or-H). Civic trust. |
| **Session fits the phone** | Pocket exists because 30-min table games do not. | Default under 90s |
| **Collect without combat** | Pocket’s door is the pack. Live/PTCGO failed when they skipped it. | Collect-only. Confirmed. |

### Invert (they use it to sell; we use it to teach)

| Their lever | Our invert |
|---|---|
| Hourglasses / Gold skip the wait | **Never.** Extra pack = 5-minute swipe quiz |
| Premium pass = third daily pack | Extra pack = learning or verified volunteer |
| Wonder Pick FOMO + paid rewind | See a friend’s pull for free; no paid reroll |
| Trading gated to protect IAP | Later: gift dupes freely. Knowledge should move |
| Rarity = power in battle | Rarity = chase + daily unique, **never** poll share |
| Hidden or punishing odds | Pity + printed mix. Progression bound to learning |

### Leave on the table

- Live poll widgets (picks a horse).
- Pay-to-complete, IAP of any kind.
- 8–10 rarity tiers (noise).
- Lore seasons, battle passes, deck power.
- Skip button that skips the **back**. Skip the animation if they want; never skip literacy as the default.

## 3. Why this maneuver fits *our* cause

18–24s already live in this loop. Pocket proved the loop works **digitally, at scale, without needing a table or a duel.** State Makers proved cards can teach Israel, physically, to a paying audience.

Kalpi is the overlap: Pocket’s **door** + State Makers’ **backs** + no till.

The addiction literature (gacha / timed packs) is real. We use the same psychology on purpose (**המטרה מקדשת את האמצעים**) and put floors under it: no spend, short sessions, pity for new knowledge, extra packs only when they learn.

If a judge asks “why not a quiz app?”: because quiz apps do not become daily habits for this age, and Pocket just showed what does.

## 4. Collectible musts, knowledge on top

We do not invent a new genre. We take the **essence** of a collectible (rip ritual, pity, printed odds, card lands in a hole, rarity you screenshot, a clock that brings you back) and bind progression to **learning**.

| Collectible must | Knowledge on top |
|---|---|
| Rip is a ritual | After the pause, a short hold → **flip the back** (member / list / system). Cannot skip as default |
| Pity | New *knowledge*, not a casino withhold |
| Printed odds | Civic trust |
| Card lands in a hole | Missing slot = missing fact |
| Screenshot the rare | Share the list-slot 1/1, then they read who it is |
| Extra pack | Quiz on a card **you already pulled** |

The card back is one lesson. The quiz, commons set, and party pack are the same job.

## 5. The format is spreading — hit it now

Not only Pokémon. One Piece took the hobby’s #2. Riftbound (League of Legends) launched late 2025 and moved faster than anything Cardlogs tracks. Cyberpunk TCG set a Kickstarter games record (~**$27M**). Sports cards are still the bulk of the US physical market. The subject keeps widening: animals (**Life TCG** — holo print run = wild population), US politics (Decision 2024; Politic Packs).

State Makers is the **best local** proof (Israel history, sold out). It is not yet a worldwide hit. That is why we also say: the iron is hot *globally*. We put Israeli lists on a format people already chase.

Collectr mid-2026: tracked hobby sales **+107% in 2025**. Do not quote a single “market size $X” from a random report. Direction is enough.
