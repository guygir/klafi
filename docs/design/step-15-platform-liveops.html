What the “creator kit” actually is.

This is not a feature inside the game. It is launch help: how the first players find Klafi.

Before a public push, we ask about ten people who already have an audience (a campus page, a politics explainer, a big WhatsApp group) to post Klafi in the same 60–90 minutes. Each person gets a unique link so we can see who sent players:

https://klafi.vercel.app/?ref=maya

The “kit” is the three posts we would hand them, ready to publish — not an app they log into:

  • A vertical walk video (9:16) of one card opening.
  • An Instagram / story still (4:5).
  • A WhatsApp image they can forward.

They do not get extra cards, a dashboard, or a payout. Players who tap the link see the same game as everyone else. The ref= only marks the door they came through.

The share-binder link is a simpler version of the same idea: one URL that shows the album. The kit is only if we also want those ten people to post a video of a pull. That video waits on step 16 (pack-open animation).
