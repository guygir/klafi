## Review and rights status

- Object illustrations: internal PoC use; review visual accuracy before publication.
- Person portraits: internal PoC concepts only. A human must review likeness accuracy, publicity/privacy implications, campaign usage, and final artist/asset rights before public release.
- No press photograph, party logo, *Eretz Nehederet* asset, clip, still, performer likeness, or branded satire character is included.
- Generated art must never be presented as documentary photography.

## Crop and presentation

- Source ratio: 3:4.
- Card well: centered cover crop.
- Party identity remains the 8px pip; generated backgrounds do not become party-color washes.
- Alt text uses the depicted civic object or the named subject plus “illustrated portrait.”
