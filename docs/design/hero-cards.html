The Kalpi

הקלפי

At the kalpi you take one paper slip for one submitted list and put it in the box. Legally you vote for a closed list, not a person. If that list wins 18 seats, the first 18 names on it enter the Knesset, unless someone resigns later. The slip is secret.

Why it matters. Knowing who sits in slots 1–20 is how you know who your vote can actually seat.

The Threshold

אחוז החסימה

Since 2015 a list needs 3.25% of valid votes to take any seats. Votes for lists under the line stay valid on the tally, but they elect nobody. In 2022 the seat quota after the threshold was about 36,227 votes. Small lists near the line can move several seats between blocs.

Why it matters. A protest slip that misses 3.25% does not become a protest MK.

Ballot mark מחל

Likud · as associated ahead of filing

Israeli ballots identify lists by letters, not by a portrait. Likud has long used the letters מחל. The CEC assigns or confirms letters when lists are filed. Until 9 September 2026, treat any letter as the historically associated mark, not as the filed 2026 slip.

Why it matters. In the booth you will look for letters, not for a name you saw on television.
