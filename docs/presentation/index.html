Surveys are not turnout

They always say they will vote.

  • IDI 2026: 80–95% of first-timers intend to. That is not a forecast.
  • 2013: ~88% intended, ~92% later said they voted, official turnout was 67.8%.
  • 18–22 were already the age most likely to stay home. Official 18–24 turnout after 2021: 46.7%.
  • Assume 2026 is still low vs older adults — even if a few points higher. Then make the slip informed.

Why digital · why free

Reach, speed, no pay-to-win.

  • Physical cannot hit 600k first-timers before 27 Oct.
  • Digital numbered holos: one per identity per day. No print cap.
  • Free forever. Monetization would fight the civic goal.
  • Alpha: one pack / 24h. Eight-hour drops later, if we want more habit.

Why cards

The format is already winning.

  • Pokémon TCG Pocket — collection-first, global, daily rips.
  • State Makers / יוצרים מדינה — 98-card Israeli history set, packs of 8, holos, first edition sold out.

Growth

Reward anything that teaches or turns out.

  • Quiz on cards you own → extra pack.
  • Verified volunteer at a real kalpi → packs.
  • Influencers who already hold 18–24.
  • Public set-race (outbid.lol spectacle, nobody pays).
  • 10k NIS first-set prize = concept, legal review — or merch / a voter night.

Neutrality · ethics

Equal sets. Sourced backs. No spending.

  • Same slots, same tone, same frame. Color only in an 8px pip.
  • Facts and dated quotes. Never our joke at a camp.
  • Likeness and marks need a real-product legal pass.
  • Dopamine on purpose: short sessions, pity for new knowledge, no IAP.
  • Do not say “youth won’t vote.” Say informed turnout.
