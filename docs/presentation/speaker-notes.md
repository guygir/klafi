## Slide 2 — Cold open

**46.7 versus 74.9.** That is 2021 turnout, Knesset Research and Information Center: ages 18–24 versus 65–74. National 2022 was 70.6.

**595 to 640 thousand** first-timers. About 8.7 percent of the roll. If they turn out, that is on the order of **six seats**.

Pause. Let the seats land.

If a judge says “IDI says 91 percent will vote”: do not fold. That is **intention**. Next slide is why intention always looks like that.

---

## Slide 3 — Surveys are not turnout

2013 IDI (same house as the 2026 survey):

- Before: ~88% certain or likely to vote.
- After: ~92% said they had voted.
- Official turnout: **67.8%**.
- Ages 18–22 were already the most likely not to intend (17.7%) and most likely to report they had not voted (~15%).

Line: “We do not have a youth-intention time series that cancels 46.7. We have a pattern: surveys run hot, official youth turnout runs cold. Working assumption — 2026 is still low versus older adults, even if it ticks up. On top of that, the slip has to be informed.”

## Slide 4 — Audience insight

Gloria Mark: 150 seconds in 2004, 75 around 2012, **47 seconds** now, median **40**.

They already collect cards. Pokémon TCG Pocket is collection-first, not a battler-first hit. In Israel, State Makers sold out a 98-card history set.

“We are not inventing a format. We are pointing a format they already love at 27 October.”

## Slide 5 — The trick

Point at the diagram.

“Pack dopamine is bait. The **back of the card** is the lesson. If we ship a beautiful rip and people never flip, we shipped a casino.”

North stars: flip-to-back percent, quiz on owned cards, set completion, “I know who I’m voting for.”

## Slide 6 — Digital and free

Why not physical: reach, speed, daily drops, **no print cap** for numbered holos, **no pay-to-win**. Civic goal and monetization would fight each other. We picked a side.

8-hour packs are a later lever. Alpha is one pack per day.

## Slide 8 — Why cards

Pocket = global collect loop. State Makers = local teaching-via-collecting, first edition sold out. Kalpi = **this election**, digital, free, all lists equal.

We copy Pocket’s collect loop, not its battles. We copy State Makers’ teaching, not its price tag.

## Slide 10 — Neutrality and ethics

PoC is Likud, Democrats, Ra'am — **map spread, not the three largest**. Same counts, same tone, same frame. Color only in an 8px pip.

Likeness and marks need a real-product pass.  
Cash prize can look like a lottery.  
We use dopamine on purpose: short sessions, pity for new knowledge cards, no spending.

**If someone flinches at AI art:** “We know this room. We are not claiming AI is more original. Lists file 9 September. Election is 27 October. Virality needs weeks. Hand-painting every MK is how you miss the vote. The cause is the deadline.”

Forbidden: “IDI proves they will vote” and “youth will not vote, period.” Say: **official youth turnout has been low; surveys overstate; assume the gap holds; make the slip informed.**
