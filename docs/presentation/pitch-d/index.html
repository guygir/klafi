  • Knesset RIC after 2021. National 2022: 70.6%. A 28-point age gap — persistent, not a collapse. Lavi & Adler: young Israelis vote more than young Europeans; the youth rate has been rising; they still vote far below older Israelis.
  • 595–640k first-timers (18–22). ~8.7% of the roll. Points / JPost + IDI citing CBS. If they turn out, on the order of six Knesset seats.
  • They are still the target because they are the lowest turnout, the largest new bloc, and the weakest list-knowledge. Older voters already show up. This group can move six seats without knowing slots 1–20.
  • Calendar we build to: lists 9 Sept · election 27 Oct · virality needs weeks. A launch the week of the vote is too late.

STRATEGY · Surveys are not turnout · two jobs

80–95% intend to vote. That is not a forecast.

  • IDI May 2026, first-timers, n=576, ages 18–22: 80–95% intend. Same institute, 2013: ~88% intended (72.8% + 15%), ~92% later said they voted, official turnout 67.8%.
  • In 2013, ages 18–22 were already most likely to stay home (17.7% intend-not / ~15% after), vs 7–9% in older groups. Intention + “I voted” overreport is known (social desirability; Waismel-Manor & Sarid 2011).
  • Working assumption: 2026 youth turnout stays low vs older adults, even if a few points above 46.7%. Do not say “they will not vote, period.” Do not say “IDI proves 91% will.”
  • Two jobs: get them there, and make the slip informed. Feed is #1 political source (IDI 2026); most do not seek. We do not invent “X% cannot name three parties.” Say: they do not know the list.

PRODUCT · Quiz on pulled cards · extra pack = learning

You only get asked what you already ripped. Prefer the last pack.

  1. Pack lands a face. You had a chance to flip the back.
  2. Popup: that same face + name. Swipe: what do they do / known for (options from the back). Swipe: which list. 3–4 options each. 5:00.
  3. Both right → one extra pack. Wrong or timeout → no pack, show the back again. One successful extra-pack quiz per day. New popup later if they fail.
  • Cannot farm a card you never pulled. Rip = chance to learn. Quiz = check. Always dismissible. Never blocks the daily pack. No “who is good.” No opinion items.
  • Commons quiz the same way: 120 / 3.25% / closed list / CEC date. Login = habit pack. Learning = bonus pack.

PRODUCT · Three choice mechanics · do not mix them · energy is dead

1. In-game faction — no reward

Optional. Pick one real list. Anonymous tile, not a name. Each pack +1 on that list’s public tally = packs opened while factioned. Scoreboard, not a poll, not a wallet. No extra pack. No prize. The race is the game. Switch ok. Unfactioned still play (packs do not tally). Same tile size; order = CEC letter / alpha, not polls.

2. Real-life party work — big reward

Verified volunteer at a party HQ, campaign, or kalpi. Best way to become knowledgeable — you are in the room. Pays a pack bundle. Does not auto-join the in-game faction. They can still pick (or not) in the game.

3. Survey → PARTY pack — tool

Optional issue survey (not “who is a bro”). Server scores a published table, same questions for everyone. Reward: one pack of matched-list politicians only. Copy: “here is that set — then collect the others.” Not an endorsement. Never hides the rest of the binder. Never auto-factions.

There is no currency called energy. You do not earn, spend, or buy it. If a room mixes 1 and 2, stop and separate them.
