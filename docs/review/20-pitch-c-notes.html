1 — Title

Do not ask yet. “Who does not reach the kalpi — and why a pack of cards is how we get them there, knowing who they are voting for.”

2 — Voting %

46.7 vs 74.9. Knesset RIC, after 2021. National 2022: 70.6%.

Stand still. This is the background.

3 — Why that number matters

Not a collapse story. Lavi & Adler: young Israelis vote more than young Europeans; the youth rate has been rising. The issue is the gap with older Israelis. A different electorate.

4 — Why it matters this year

595–640k first-timers. 8.7%. On the order of six seats. 27 October.

5 — “They say they will vote”

IDI 2026: 80–95% intend. Then the 2013 same-institute pattern: ~88% intended, ~92% claimed they voted, 67.8% official. 18–22 were already the most likely to stay home.

Do not treat 91% as a forecast.

6 — Working assumption

Official youth turnout has been low. 2026 will still be low versus older adults, even if a few points higher. Second job: informed slip — a smart vote.

7 — Two rooms that do not know

Lived, not a stat. Do not invent a “X% cannot name three parties” number.

  1. Me: “Bennett is a bro.” That is a vibe. That is not a platform.
  2. My little brother — does not know who to vote for. This is the young-voter problem in one person.
  3. Hadar Muchtar — same not-knowing.

“This is the product. Not a survey.”

8 — The young-voter problem (brother)

Say it as patterns, not insults:

  • Many are new. They may default to the parents’ vote.
  • They may not care — war, load, enough already.
  • TikTok is full of viral fake news. That is often their civics class.
  • Some are afraid to show an opinion (at least on one side). The game later lets them pledge a faction anonymously.

9 — Why not parents

Two reasons. Do not skip the second.

  1. Rebellion. A parent lecture is the wrong teacher for this age.
  2. Parents are invested and emotional. They already decided. That is a fight, not a syllabus.

10 — The solution is אוריינות פוליטית

North star. A smart vote.

“How do you teach them to vote properly?” Not a parent. Not a party ad. Not a TikTok. Knowledge they chose to flip.

TikTok + AI fake news is another cause that supports this solution: if they know who is on the list and what the list actually says, a fake has less room. We do not fact-check clip-by-clip. We give them knowledge.

Jam goals in one breath if asked: knowledge, social involvement, encourage voting, taking a choice, literacy, fight fake news via knowledge, young audience.

* Aside — do not put on a slide

I think this product would help the Democrats more, because when people do not know how to vote they mostly default anti-Democrat.

Personal read. Not the thesis. Not a design input. The game stays equal-structure and never steers. If a judge smells a side, we already lost.

11 — Why they are still the target

Say the three reasons out loud:

  1. Lowest turnout — 46.7% vs 74.9%.
  2. Largest new bloc — ~600k, six seats.
  3. Weakest list-knowledge — feed civics, not platforms.

Older voters already show up. This group is the one the election can still lose — and the one that can vote without knowing the list.

12 — What characterizes them

47 seconds (Gloria Mark). Dopamine. Viral or invisible. Age of wars and TikTok.

If the product is a long civic explainer, we already lost.

13 — Local proof: State Makers / יוצרים מדינה

Do not swallow this into “they already collect cards.” Say the details:

  • State Makers / יוצרים מדינה, by Piece of History.
  • 98-card Israeli history set. Packs of 8. Holos.
  • First edition sold out.
  • People paid to collect — and learned the country’s story.
  • Link if they ask: pieceofhistory.com/statemakers

“The format already teaches Israel, physically, to a paying audience. We point it at this election. Digital. Free.”

Then the objection: “that is cardboard. Why a phone game?”

14 — Five things we take from Pocket

Count them on your fingers. This is the digital maneuver, not a vibe:

  1. Daily timed pack — a clock, not a pile. They come back.
  2. The rip is a ritual — tear, sound, pause. Addictive on purpose.
  3. Collecting sets is the progression — the card lands in a hole in the binder.
  4. Rarity is a screenshot — the holo is spectacle. That is the virality.
  5. Quick session — a bus ride, not a 30-minute match.

Scale if asked: ~150M downloads, ~$1.3B year one. Earlier digital Pokémon skipped collecting and stalled.

We take those five. We do not take the shop. Extra pack is a 5-minute swipe quiz. Battles optional — we skip them.

“If a judge asks why not a quiz app: quiz apps do not become a daily habit for this age. Pocket just showed what does.”

14b — The iron is hot (STRATEGY)

One breath. Do not turn this into a market-size lecture.

Tracked hobby sales doubled in 2025 (Collectr). Pokémon still first. One Piece took second. New: Riftbound, Cyberpunk TCG (~$27M Kickstarter). Sports cards never left. US already makes politics cards. Life TCG even prints animals to population.

State Makers is the best local proof. The world already collects everything. Hit the iron while it’s hot.

If they want the longer version, that is Pitch D.

15 — Trait → game

Map it with your finger:

  • Short attention → 90-second rip
  • Dopamine → daily pack, holos
  • Feed politics → the back of the card
  • Afraid to speak → anonymous in-game faction (a tally, not a currency). Real-life party work is a different, large reward.
  • Viral → share the pull, race the set (nobody pays)

“The rip is bait. The back is the lesson. Extra packs come from a 5-minute swipe. A short survey can unlock a party pack — politicians from the matched list only, as a tool, not an endorsement. Show-up is the outcome.”

Kalpi never picks a side. You may.

16 — outbid.lol

Link it. outbid.lol · about.

August 2026: a public ladder where rank is what you pay. Founder-reported ~1M+ visits, six-figure bids, top spot on the order of $13–17k. Companies paid to be on top to get famous.

Proof: people and companies will spend (waste) a lot of money to go viral.

Our invert: same spectator sport — a public race — for a good cause. Nobody pays. Faction tally and set completion, not a till.

Treat the dollar counts as founder-reported if a judge is picky. The existence of the board is enough.

17 — Therefore Kalpi

Name, constraints. Free. Collect-only. Equal sets. Anonymous camp. The game does not pick a side.

17b — Bait vs goal (PRODUCT) — do not skip

This is the thesis.

“The pack is bait. The goal is to teach Israeli politics so they vote — a smart vote, and they show up.”

The card back is one example. Not the whole product. Quiz on a face they already pulled. Commons. Party pack. Trading a dupe so someone else reads a back.

Progression is bound to learning. Extra pack = quiz on a card you already pulled. In-game faction is a scoreboard, not a pack. Real-life party work is the large reward.

18 — Play it

90 seconds. Kalpi flip. Threshold holo + date. מחל = letters in the booth.

Alpha: pack, flip, binder, 28 sourced cards.

If they ask implementation: HTML for approachability. The server pulls the pack. The player cannot inject what they drew. That is baseline trust, not a pentest.

19 — Progression is learning

Image, name, two swipes, five minutes. Role + list. Both right → extra pack.

Also designed: survey → party pack; streaks; set chase; numbered holos. Faction + a $$ contest are two hooks, not the whole loop.

If they ask about news/polls: we may later show CEC facts (filings, letters). We do not put live polls in our voice. That would pick a horse.

20 — Empty of lore + QR

המטרה מקדשת את האמצעים. Jams reward heart in the fiction. We shipped dopamine on purpose. AI art, no story bible, a party energy board. The civic goal is the message. Say it before the room calls it soulless.

Hold the QR. It goes to guygir.itch.io — prior jam games. I have shipped jams. This time the emptiness is a choice, not a missing soul.

21 — Close (the only ask)

Do not split judges vs partners. One line the room can finish:

“If this should leave the room, it needs to be live in September — free, Hebrew, seeded where 18–24 already are.”

Then: “Assume the gap. Give them a pack they will open.”

Stop.
