Kalpi — Story deck speaker notes (~9 minutes + rip)

This is version B: a story. Version A is the product-argument deck at ../index.html.

Open index.html. Arrow keys. Citations: ../citations.md.

Demo tabs: hero-cards.html, wireframes.html.

1 — Title

Do not explain the game yet. One line:

“This is a story about who does not reach the kalpi — and a pack of cards that might change that.”

2 — The last official number

46.7% of 18–24s voted after 2021. 74.9% of 65–74s did. Knesset Research and Information Center.

Stand still. Do not jump to the product.

3 — That is the issue

The national rate in 2022 was 70.6%. Young adults are not “a bit lower.” They are a different electorate.

Lavi & Adler: they vote more than young Europeans, and the youth rate has been rising. We are not inventing a collapse. We are pointing at a persistent age gap.

4 — Why it matters this year

595 to 640 thousand first-timers. About 8.7% of the roll. On the order of six seats. Election day 27 October 2026.

Pause on six seats.

5 — “But they say they will vote”

IDI May 2026, first-timers 18–22: 80–95% intend to vote.

Read it as a headline someone will throw at you. Then: “That number, alone, does not mean October.”

6 — Surveys always look like that

Same institute, 2013:

  • ~88% intended (72.8% certain + 15% probably).
  • Afterward, ~92% said they had voted.
  • Official turnout: 67.8%.
  • Ages 18–22 were already the group most likely to say they would stay home (17.7%) and most likely to report they had not voted (~15%).

This is not a 2026 miracle. It is how vote questions behave — social desirability, politically involved samples. Israeli municipal work shows the same overreport.

7 — So we assume the gap holds

We do not have a perfect youth-intention time series. We have official 18–24 turnout that was low, and surveys that always run hot.

Working assumption: 2026 youth turnout is still low versus older adults and versus the country — even if it is a few points above 46.7.

Second job, same people: the slip has to be informed.

8 — Who they are

18–24. Especially first-timers. Feed is the #1 political source. Most do not seek politics. They distrust parties. They do not do organized activism.

They do not live in manifesto PDFs.

9 — What characterizes them

Gloria Mark: 47 seconds on a screen (median 40). Age of wars and TikTok. Variable rewards. Things go viral or they do not exist.

If it is not a daily dopamine loop, it is a civic lecture they will not finish.

10 — What already works on them

Pokémon TCG Pocket: collection-first, daily rips, global.

State Makers / יוצרים מדינה: 98 Israeli history cards, packs of 8, holos, first edition sold out.

We are not inventing cards. We are pointing a format they already love at this election.

11 — Therefore Kalpi

Free digital collect-only TCG of the lists. Daily pack. Flip the back.

Bait = rip, holo, numbered date, race the set.
Product = members, letters, planks, show up.

If they never flip, we shipped a casino. If they never reach 27 Oct, we shipped trivia.

12 — Live rip

90 seconds. Kalpi card → flip. Threshold holo, date stamp → flip. מחל → “in the booth you look for letters.” Binder dashed wells = missing knowledge.

13 — Why the art is AI

Look at the room. This community often hates AI art. Say it first.

“We are not claiming AI is more original. We are claiming the calendar. Lists file 9 September. Election is 27 October. Virality needs weeks. Hand-painting every member of every list is how you miss the vote. The cause is the deadline. If a later partner funds an artist, we take it. We do not wait.”

14 — Close

Equal sets. No paywall. Reward learning and real volunteering. Public race, nobody pays.

Official youth turnout has been 46.7%. Surveys will always say 90. Assume the gap. Then give them a pack they will actually open — and a back they will actually read — before 27 October.

Stop.
