# Kalpi — Source list

Use these in speaker notes and card footnotes. Do not cite social posts as facts.

## Turnout and audience

- Knesset Research and Information Center, information page on official turnout by demographics, 12 Sept 2022. After 2021: **46.7% ages 18–24** vs **74.9% ages 65–74**.  
  https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/a535249a-a802-ed11-814e-005056aa4246/2_a535249a-a802-ed11-814e-005056aa4246_11_19630.pdf
- 2022 national turnout **70.6%**. Israel Democracy Institute, 2022 election page.  
  https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/elections/2022/
- IDI, “First-Time Voters Ahead of the 26th Knesset Elections,” May 2026 survey (n=576, ages 18–22). Intention 80–95%; voting meaningful >80%; ~two-thirds interested; digital sphere as main information source; low organized activism.  
  https://en.idi.org.il/articles/64746  
  Hebrew: https://www.idi.org.il/articles/64725
- IDI, “Who Doesn’t Vote in the Israeli Knesset Elections?” 2013 cycle: **~88%** intended to vote (72.8% certain + 15% probably); post-election **~92%** said they had voted; official turnout **67.8%**. Ages **18–22** were the most likely not to intend (**17.7%**) and most likely to report not voting (**~15%**).  
  https://en.idi.org.il/articles/5204
- Turnout overreport is a known survey bias (social desirability, politically involved samples), including Israeli municipal evidence: Waismel-Manor & Sarid, *IJPOR* 2011.
- First-time cohort size: IDI / CBS ~**640,000**; Points Location Intelligence / JPost ~**595,000** (~8.7% of electorate). Use the range.
- Lavi & Adler (Tel Aviv–Yaffo Academic College), via Ynet: young Israelis vote less than older Israelis, more than young Europeans; youth turnout rising.

## Attention and format

- Gloria Mark, UC Irvine / APA *Speaking of Psychology* (Feb 2023); *Attention Span* (2023). Screen focus **2.5 min (2004) → 75s (~2012) → ~47s recent; median 40s**.  
  https://www.apa.org/news/podcasts/speaking-of-psychology/attention-spans  
  https://ics.uci.edu/2023/01/26/regaining-focus-in-a-world-of-digital-distractions/
- Pokémon TCG Pocket launch (collection-first), 29–30 Oct 2024.  
  https://press.pokemon.com/en/Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Pocket-Launches-Today-Offering-a-Fresh-Take-
- Pocket year-one scale (downloads / revenue): PocketGamer.biz; ~150M downloads, ~$1.3B. Collection-first, not battle-first.
- Naavik: Pocket gives packs on **daily login**, not as a match reward; shows where a new card **slots into the collection**.  
  https://naavik.co/digest/pokemon-tcg-collects-some-new-tricks/
- Cut or Tap (Gearhart, Nov 2024): earlier digital Pokémon abandoned collecting and failed to grow mobile; Pocket is for people who never played the physical TCG; phone sessions vs 30-min table games.  
  https://www.cutortap.com/2024/11/the-game-design-behind-pokemon-tcg-pocket/
- Pocket rip ritual (swipe, sound designed from real packs): Arcader / Creatures art director Satoru Nagaya.  
  https://arcader.org/news/how-pokemon-tcg-pocket-replicates-the-feel-of-opening-a-physical-card-pack/
- Digital rip hygiene (show odds, ritual, second beat after the open): Blez “Pick, Rip, Reveal.”  
  https://medium.com/avianu/pick-rip-reveal-designing-blez-online-a6b48adab35d
- Kalpi take/leave table: [digital-collectibles.md](../product/digital-collectibles.md).
- **State Makers / יוצרים מדינה** — Piece of History. Israeli history TCG: **98 cards**, packs of **8**, holos; first edition **sold out**. Local proof the format teaches. Do not drop from the pitch.  
  https://www.pieceofhistory.com/statemakers
