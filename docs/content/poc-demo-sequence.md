# Kalpi PoC — reproducible six-card demo

This is the fixed demonstration journey for review and presentations. It uses only the existing 28-card core set. Prestige and Satire remain final-product backlog ideas and are not part of the PoC.

The demo preserves the published pack composition: **3 Common · 2 Uncommon · 1 Rare**.

## Sequence

1. **SYS-C-02 — The Kalpi** (`Uncommon`, approved)  
   Opens with the ballot-box mechanic and the rule that a voter chooses a list.
2. **SYS-C-01 — The Knesset** (`Common`, approved)  
   Establishes the 120-seat, one-constituency system.
3. **LIK-P-02 — “Broad national government”** (`Common`, fact-checked)  
   Introduces the distinction between a campaign frame and coalition arithmetic.
4. **DEM-P-01 — October 7 state commission of inquiry** (`Common`, fact-checked)  
   Introduces one concrete current-opposition agenda item.
5. **DEM-M-02 — Naama Lazimi** (`Uncommon`, draft)  
   Connects a party position to a realistic list slot and a person a vote may seat.
6. **DEM-Q-01 — Quote, Yair Golan** (`Rare`, draft)  
   Ends with the supported side’s own closing ask, followed by full context and source status.

## Editorial shape

`ballot mechanic → seat system → government claim → opposition action → list member → chair’s closing ask`

The first two cards orient. The middle pair creates a claim/action contrast. The final two connect the disclosed advocacy case to actual list placement and a sourced quotation.

## Open publication blockers

- `DEM-M-02` needs a direct primary-results or party-list source instead of an index.
- `DEM-Q-01` needs the direct article, transcript, recording, or official publication containing the complete quotation.
- Any generated or placeholder person art remains a PoC asset until a human rights review.

These blockers remain visible in Studio. They do not prevent a labeled internal PoC demonstration.
